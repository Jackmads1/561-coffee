Why 561?

561 is where it started. The name is a nod to home, and a promise to make coffee the people there would be proud of.

We started 561 Coffee because good coffee shouldn't feel complicated. We buy from farms we can name, roast light enough to taste where it came from, and serve it without the attitude.
